Cordillera Administrative Region

The highlands of the Philippines

Abra

A landlocked province known for its weaving traditions, the Abra River, and the historic capital of Bangued. It is celebrated for its vibrant Ilocano-Tingguian heritage and lush river valleys.

Apayao

Home to dense rainforests, the Apayao River, and the indigenous Isneg people. A frontier province of pristine nature, untouched waterfalls, and rich biodiversity.

Benguet

Known as the "Salad Bowl of the Philippines," Benguet is rich in minerals, cool-climate vegetables, and the iconic strawberry farms of La Trinidad in the highlands.

Ifugao

Home to the 2,000-year-old Banaue Rice Terraces — a UNESCO World Heritage Site carved into the mountains by the Ifugao people, reflecting ancient engineering and culture.

Kalinga

A province of warriors and weavers, famous for the traditional Kalinga tattoo art (batok) and the Chico River cutting through its dramatic, rugged mountain terrain.

Mountain Province

Where the ancient Sagada hanging coffins cling to limestone cliffs. A land of caves, misty peaks, and the living Kankana-ey culture, drawing adventurers from across the world.
